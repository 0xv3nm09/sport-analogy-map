# Contributing

Thanks for helping Americans learn cricket through the sports they know.

- One comparison per entry: `like` (the resemblance), `except` (where it
  breaks), `numbers_in_text` (every number your text contains), and
  `reviewed_by` (empty until approved).
- You may propose entries; a fan of the target sport approves them. If you
  wrote it, someone else reviews it (maker-checker).
- Keep `like`/`except` under about 25 words each, plain language, no league
  or team trademarks (no NFL, MLB, NBA, FIFA, ICC...).
- No player criticism, no betting language, no politics: lines built from
  these entries are read aloud to families.
- JSON must validate against `schema/comparison.schema.json`.
